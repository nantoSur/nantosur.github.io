NORZ Softphone — Privacy Policy

Version: 1.1.7  |  Effective: 2026-11-24

NORZ is a SIP desktop softphone. This policy explains what data the app accesses, stores and transmits.

Data we (the developer) collect

None. NORZ does not send analytics, telemetry, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or usage data to us. There is no account with the developer and no central server operated by us.

The app itself does access and transmit the data described below, but only to servers that you configure yourself.

Data stored on your device

DataPurposeLocation
SIP username, password, server/domainTo register the SIP account and place/receive callsLocal app settings (NORZ.ini)
Call history (numbers, time, duration)To show the local call logcall_log.db
CRM contacts you addTo provide click-to-call from crm.htmlBrowser localStorage on your machine
Remote phonebook URL you enterTo load contacts/BLF from your PBXLocal app settings
UI theme and optionsTo remember your preferencesLocal app settings

The saved SIP password is encrypted locally using the Windows CryptoAPI. You can delete all of this at any time: use Logout → Yes in the app to remove saved login, call log and phonebook, or uninstall the app.

Data sent to other services

NORZ connects only to servers you configure yourself:

  • Your SIP server / PBX (for registration, voice calls, presence/BLF).
  • An optional remote phonebook URL that you enter in Settings.

Audio from your microphone is sent directly to the configured SIP endpoint when you start or answer a call. This build does not support video and does not use the webcam. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Permissions

  • Microphone — required for voice calls.
  • Network (internet/intranet) — required for SIP signaling and media.
  • Protocol registration (sip:, tel:, callto:, msip:) — so clicking a number in a browser, e-mail or CRM opens NORZ.
  • Full trust (runFullTrust) — required because NORZ is a classic Win32 desktop application that uses the Windows SIP stack, local files and the system tray.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact information provided in your Microsoft Store listing.
